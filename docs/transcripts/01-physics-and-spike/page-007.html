While a slider moves, and for a second after, the part it acts on glows ruby: the regulator pointer, pins and F/S marks; the stud and its track; the eight timing screws; the poise cut; the gravity glyph when you change position. The wind slider has no drawn part, so it shows the swing instead: two arcs outside the rim from the notch centre to each extreme, with ghost pins at the ends, so dragging through the day visibly shrinks the swing from ±280° to ±250°. Direct link to see it frozen: ?state=visual-wind-swing.

Your question about the position selector on puzzle 4. It is the diagnostic tool, not a fix. A poise error only shows when gravity pulls across the balance's axis, so:

  • Dial up and dial down read clean. That's the "fine on the nightstand" symptom.
  • The four crown positions each put the heavy spot at a different angle to gravity. The error flips sign between crown up and crown down and vanishes in the two positions where the heavy spot sits level. Comparing the four readings is how a watchmaker locates the heavy spot before touching the balance.
  • Vertical positions also lose about 30° of amplitude from pivot friction, which is why the readout drops to 250°.

So the intended play is: cycle the positions, see which gain and which lose, work out the rim angle, then remove weight there with the poising control and confirm all four verticals agree with dial up. The puzzle text doesn't yet say this plainly; that copy belongs to the onboarding lane, and I'll add the request to the handoffs file so it gets explained in the goal line.
